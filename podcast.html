Siin avaldatavad vestlused on minu soov tuua laiema avalikkuseni Eesti masinõppe tippspetsialistide teadmised. Räägin inimestega, kellel on sügav arusaamine masinõppe meetoditest ja kes on suutnud neid edukalt päriselu probleemide lahendamisel rakendada. Arutame, millist mõju tehisintellekt meie ühiskonnale juba avaldab, millist mõju see võib tulevikus avaldada ning kuidas selle kõigega toime tulla.

With these conversations, I want to bring insights from Estonia's leading machine learning specialists to a wider audience. I talk to people who understand machine learning methods deeply and have successfully applied them to real problems. We discuss the effects AI is already having on society, what effects it might have in the future, and how to respond.

The recordings are in Estonian.
